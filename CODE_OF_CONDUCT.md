## Government Professional Standards

### Security Clearance Responsibilities

#### Classified Information Handling
All personnel with security clearances must:

- **Protect Classified Information:** Safeguard all classified information according to government standards
- **Need-to-Know Principle:** Access classified information only when necessary for assigned duties
- **Proper Storage:** Store classified materials in approved containers and facilities
- **Secure Communication:** Use only approved communication methods for classified discussions
- **Report Security Incidents:** Immediately report any security violations or suspicious activities

#### Personal Conduct Standards
Security clearance holders must maintain exemplary personal conduct:

- **Financial Responsibility:** Maintain good financial standing and report financial difficulties
- **Foreign Contacts:** Report foreign contacts and travel as required by security regulations
- **Substance Abuse:** Abstain from illegal drug use and report alcohol-related incidents
- **Criminal Activity:** Report any arrests, charges, or legal issues immediately
- **Personal Relationships:** Disclose relationships that could create conflicts of interest

#### Government Customer Interactions
**Service Excellence:**

- Protect government information and interests

**Cultural Sensitivity:**

- Adapt communication style to audience needs
- Show appreciation for public service and sacrifice
- Support government mission objectives

## Ethical Standards and Integrity

### Government Ethics Compliance

#### Conflict of Interest
All personnel must avoid conflicts of interest:

**Financial Conflicts:**
- Disclose financial interests that could affect judgment
- Avoid investments in competing organizations
- Report gifts or benefits from government customers
- Maintain arm's length relationships with vendors
- Seek guidance when conflicts may exist

**Professional Conflicts:**
- Avoid outside employment that conflicts with duties
- Disclose relationships with competitors or partners
- Maintain objectivity in decision-making
- Avoid using position for personal advantage
- Report potential conflicts immediately

#### Whistleblower Protection
We provide robust protection for ethical reporting:

**Protected Activities:**
- Reporting violations of law, regulation, or policy
- Disclosing waste, fraud, or abuse
- Reporting security violations or concerns
- Raising safety or health issues
- Cooperating with authorized investigations

**Reporting Channels:**
- **Ethics Hotline:** 1-855-AVEROX-ETHICS (anonymous reporting available)
- **Government Inspector General:** Direct reporting to appropriate IG office
- **Ethics Officer:** ethics@averox.com (confidential consultation)
- **Legal Counsel:** legal@averox.com (privileged communication)
- **Human Resources:** hr@averox.com (internal resolution)

### Information Security Ethics

#### Sensitive Information Protection
**Classification Handling:**
- Properly mark and handle classified information
- Protect sensitive but unclassified information
- Follow government information sharing guidelines
- Report information spillage incidents immediately
- Maintain information security awareness

**Personal Information Privacy:**
- Protect personally identifiable information (PII)
- Follow Privacy Act requirements for government records
- Respect individual privacy rights and expectations
- Report privacy breaches or concerns promptly
- Maintain confidentiality of personal information

#### Cybersecurity Responsibilities
**Individual Accountability:**
- Use strong, unique passwords for all systems
- Enable multi-factor authentication where available
- Keep software and systems updated with security patches
- Report suspicious emails, websites, or activities
- Participate in required cybersecurity training

**Organizational Security:**
- Follow established security policies and procedures
- Protect physical and digital assets
- Report security incidents and vulnerabilities
- Support security awareness and training programs
- Maintain security clearance eligibility

All personnel must maintain continuous security awareness:

**Daily Security Practices:**
- Verify identities before sharing sensitive information
- Use secure communication channels for sensitive discussions
- Lock computers and secure documents when leaving workspace
- Report lost or stolen equipment immediately
- Maintain situational awareness of security threats

**Threat Recognition:**
- Identify social engineering attempts
- Recognize phishing and malicious emails
- Understand physical security risks
- Report suspicious activities or individuals
- Stay informed about current threat landscape

**Individual Response:**
- Stop potentially harmful activities immediately
- Preserve evidence without tampering
- Report incidents through established channels
- Cooperate fully with security investigations
- Learn from incidents to prevent recurrence

**Team Response:**
- Support incident response team activities
- Communicate clearly and accurately about incidents
- Follow established procedures and protocols
- Maintain confidentiality of ongoing investigations
- Participate in post-incident lessons learned sessions

- Be cautious about information shared in public settings
- Protect schedules and travel information
- Limit disclosure of technical details
- Consider adversary collection capabilities

**Professional Networking:**
- Verify identities of professional contacts
- Be cautious about information shared at conferences
- Protect customer and project information
- Report suspicious recruitment attempts
- Maintain awareness of foreign intelligence threats

## Enforcement and Accountability

### Violation Reporting and Investigation

#### Reporting Mechanisms
**Multiple Reporting Channels:**
- **Direct Supervisor:** First-line reporting for most issues
- **Human Resources:** HR@averox.com or 1-855-AVEROX-HR
- **Ethics Hotline:** 1-855-AVEROX-ETHICS (anonymous options available)
- **Government Inspector General:** Direct reporting for serious violations
- **Legal Counsel:** legal@averox.com for legal issues

**Anonymous Reporting:**
- Online portal: https://averox.com/ethics/report
- Phone hotline: 1-855-AVEROX-ETHICS
- Mail: Averox Ethics Office, P.O. Box [REDACTED]
- Third-party service: Available 24/7 in multiple languages

#### Investigation Process
**Fair and Thorough Process:**
1. **Initial Assessment:** Determine scope and severity of alleged violation
2. **Investigation Planning:** Assign appropriate investigators and resources
3. **Evidence Collection:** Gather facts through interviews and document review
4. **Analysis and Conclusions:** Determine whether violations occurred
5. **Corrective Action:** Implement appropriate disciplinary or corrective measures
6. **Follow-up:** Monitor to ensure effective resolution and prevent recurrence

**Due Process Protections:**
- Right to representation during investigations
- Opportunity to respond to allegations
- Appeals process for disciplinary actions
- Protection against retaliation for good faith reporting
- Confidentiality protection during investigation process

### Consequences for Violations

#### Progressive Discipline
**Disciplinary Actions (depending on severity):**
- **Verbal Counseling:** For minor violations requiring correction
- **Written Warning:** For repeated minor violations or single moderate violations
- **Suspension:** For serious violations requiring temporary removal
- **Termination:** For severe violations or repeated serious violations
- **Criminal Referral:** For violations that may constitute criminal activity

**Security Clearance Actions:**
- **Suspension:** Temporary removal of clearance during investigation
- **Revocation:** Permanent removal of security clearance
- **Incident Reports:** Notification to government security offices
- **Reciprocal Actions:** Coordination with other government agencies
- **Appeals Process:** Right to appeal security clearance actions

#### Factors Considered
**Mitigating Factors:**
- Voluntary disclosure of violations
- Cooperation with investigation
- Acceptance of responsibility
- No prior disciplinary history
- Circumstances beyond individual control

**Aggravating Factors:**
- Intentional violations or misconduct
- Attempts to cover up violations
- Impact on national security or public safety
- Pattern of repeated violations
- Failure to cooperate with investigation
